# doc-26 (part slabs, copy on write in the VM process): fs-bench A/B on ubu1 (board 6212), 2026-11-22

Board 6212 measures boards 6204-6210 against the commit before them. Each side was built in a tree
and target of its own. The rounds ran in one `hotbed run --bench` command on ubu1, through
`scripts/lib/bench-ab-linux-host.sh`: 3 rounds per side, interleaved. Each round that others' disk
writes contaminated ran again once (`CLADEFS_AB_RERUN=1`), for 5 rounds per side. Cases: bench-fs-fxfs-guest-dax and
bench-fork-fxfs-guest-dax-cold/-warm, with bench-fs-ext4-guest-kernel as the control; groups
io, files, fsync, sync; 2 iterations each. Exact counts come from `scripts/counts.sh`
(`CLADEFS_AB_COUNTS=1`). The raw output is in
[ubu1-doc26-ab-2026-11-22/](ubu1-doc26-ab-2026-11-22/), with the A/B table in its `ab.md`.

- **A** = `e7a694cb1` (the commit before board 6204), with two cherry-picks that aren't doc-26:
  - `06e2b03e7` (board 6283), without which its guest harness doesn't build;
  - `1e7ad5ce3` (board 6400), without which every fork-cold round fails. The fork's daemon starts
    on an empty bucket of its own, `ByBucket::own_number` caches `None`, and opening the fork's
    cell in bucket 1 is refused with `EACCES`.
- **B** = `0427f633c`: main with boards 6204-6210.

The range also holds other work merged meanwhile (bucket and cache work in
932948311, guest Fxfs barriers in 8e870f0c2, relocation commits in 75ac5f4d4, and others). So a
count that differs is doc-26's only if doc-26's code explains it.

## Timings: not judged

Both runs that day were contaminated: others wrote to rpool beside most rounds, against the bound

- **First run:** 8 of 12 rounds left out.
- **Second run** (this one): 3 of 5 A rounds and 4 of 5 B rounds left out. The writers were other

workspaces' pool: board 6887. The timings get rerun once that is fixed, in board 7082.

Counts that moved, in every round of both fork cases, outside doc-26's code:

| Counter | A | B | B/A |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cladefsd-commits` | 64 | 114 | 1.78× |
| `guest-read-requests` (= `cladefsd-reads` - 2) | 2518 | 820 | 0.33× |
| `cladefsd-read-bytes` (fork-cold) | 16.9 MB | 10.0 MB | 0.60× |
| `cladefsd-page-ins` (fork-cold) | 3926 | 3064 | 0.78× |

The guest's reads and commits are its Fxfs's, not the window's. Board 7080 found the cause:
8e870f0c2 (board 6011, guest Fxfs `barriers_enabled`). On ubu1, A with only 8e870f0c2 added goes
from 32 to 57 commits per fork, and from about 1265 to 410 guest reads. The guest's 5 commit
requests per fork are the same on both. Each journal write Fxfs flags `PRE_BARRIER` is sent as
`WRITE_BARRIER`, which the daemon serves with an ordered commit before the write
(`OpenCell::write_with_barriers`). That ordered commit doesn't wait for the disk. It is how
barriers keep the journal's order without per-4 KiB checksums or recorded flushes, which is
why the reads fell. Intended: in A/B's numbers, the extra commits cost about 0.5 ms of commit
store time per run, while reads per fork fell to a third.

`counts.sh`'s other steps:
- **lifecycle:** passes on both sides. Its request counts moved from 932948311 (one head slot: a
  cold copy-open is 4 requests, not 22; a cold fork 6, not 33).
- **gc-deleted and gc-clones:** pass on both sides.
- **fork-memory:** fails on A in both builds, by 80 bytes. On B it passed in the first build and
  failed in the second, by 4096 bytes. The heap after the forks are opened and closed again is
  off by a page, sometimes: board 7078.

## Verdict

doc-26's counts show no regression: no stage maps or private pages added, and as many faults,
maps and private requests as before. Its timings are unjudged until board 6887 (rerun: board
7082).
